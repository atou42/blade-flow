# Subagent Synthesis

## Screening Rules

The content pools were generated broadly and then filtered aggressively.

Keep effects that are readable at high speed, felt immediately in combat, tied to the four-direction input language, and able to create a distinct run identity.

Reject effects that are pure stat bumps, require long memory chains, invert or confuse controls, hide important combat information, make normal runs stressful, or turn the game into a traditional RPG inventory screen.

## Relic Pass

Three subagents generated about ninety-five relic candidates across route relics, stacking relics, and strange relics.

The screened pool keeps thirty-seven relics. The final shape is mostly route relics, with a smaller amount of stacking relics and a small group of strange or boss relics.

Strong retained examples include Redline Scabbard, Floating Point, Guard Point, Drop Hammer, Combo Lantern, Mirror Scar, Iron Encore, Guillotine Weather, and Blood Cinema.

## Talent Pass

Three subagents generated about one hundred talent candidates across long-term progression, in-run route shaping, and mastery challenges.

The screened pool keeps forty-eight talents. The retained talents focus on starting options, reward control, training, route preference, optional challenges, fixed seeds, and post-combat analysis.

Strong retained examples include Starter Draft, First Cut Tutor, Reward Filter, Variant Bias, Speed Draft, Air Marshal, Break Doctrine, Form Commitment, Thin Blade Vow, Full Compass, Flicksmith, and Grandmaster Seed.

## Equipment Pass

Five subagents generated about two hundred equipment candidates across weapon styles, utility gear, buildcraft gear, high-risk gear, and character-style sets.

The screened pool keeps seventy-two equipment entries. Equipment is intentionally larger than the relic and talent pools because it defines starting identity.

Strong retained examples include Storm Katana, Gravity Hammer, Gunblade, Mirror Fan, Resonance Blade, Twin Needle, Halo Chakram, Four-Wind Jian, Miststep Cloak, Compass Engine, Duelist's Scabbard, Skyhook Rig, Needle Compass, Ratchet Gauntlet, Glass Meteor, and The Boss's Spare Hand.
