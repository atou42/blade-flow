# Phase 2: Content Alpha

## Goal

Expand from a strong vertical slice into a replayable content alpha.

## Scope

Add more enemies, bosses, events, and room types.

Expand relics toward the screened thirty-seven relic pool.

Expand talents toward the screened forty-eight talent pool.

Expand equipment toward the screened seventy-two equipment pool.

Add daily seeds, optional oaths, training room, combo notebook, and post-combat breakdown.

## Success Criteria

Runs can meaningfully differ by equipment, relic route, card upgrades, and talent bias.

The content pool supports repeated play without making the combat unreadable.

The player can understand why a run is becoming speed, control, counter, burst, or damage focused.
