# Asset Plan v0.1

Date: 2026-05-07

This pass is for visual direction validation, not final sliced production assets. The goal is to decide whether Blade Flow should continue with the water-ink blade direction before producing transparent cutouts and sprite-ready variants.

## Scope

Generate three art boards:

- `bf-v01-duel-characters.png`: player and Boss duel identity. It must show the player below, the Boss above, and a strong red Boss core.
- `bf-v01-slash-vfx.png`: four directional slash effects. It must clearly separate up launch, right chase, left counter, and down burst.
- `bf-v01-card-icons.png`: card, relic, talent, and equipment icon language. It must use the route colors without relying on text.

## Style Rules

Use sumi ink, bone white, vermilion red, black, parchment texture, and small acid green accents. Keep the images specific to Blade Flow: vertical duel, fast combo pressure, ink blade light, and Japanese action-game energy. Do not copy any existing copyrighted character, logo, or UI.

## Acceptance Criteria

Each board must be a valid PNG, at least 1024 pixels on its shortest side, and visually readable at mobile scale. The character board must show player and Boss hierarchy. The VFX board must contain at least four distinct slash shapes. The icon board must contain repeated icon candidates with visible route-color differences. Any board that is too text-heavy, too generic, or missing the Blade Flow silhouette logic fails this pass.
