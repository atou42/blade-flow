# Talent Pool

## Pool Shape

Talents are split into long-term tools, in-run route shaping, and optional mastery challenges.

They should not become raw power inflation. They should help players practice and express a style.

## Screened Talent Names

Long-term tools: Starter Draft, First Cut Tutor, Clean Hand, Practice Mark, Combo Notebook, Ghost Replay, Direction Drills, Slow Room, Combo Labels, Route Scout, Boss Study, Reward Filter, Reward Pin, Variant Bias, Upgrade Lens, Mentor Choice, Collection Case, Oath Slots, Daily Seed, Input Buffer Tuning.

In-run route shaping: Speed Draft, Chain Pursuit, Rapid Refinement, Air Marshal, Clean Arc, Gravity Lesson, Counter Doctrine, Riposte Bias, Slipstream, Return Cut, Break Doctrine, Heavy Line, Crush Window, Ender Priority, Sharp Basics, Form Commitment.

Mastery challenges: Directional Purist, Perfect Pursuit, Airborne Doctrine, Groundbreaker, Thin Blade Vow, Starter Pride, Overdrive Debt, Full Compass, Juggler's License, Risk Ledger, Flicksmith, Grandmaster Seed.

## First-Version Picks

The first version should prioritize Starter Draft, First Cut Tutor, Reward Filter, Variant Bias, Direction Drills, Combo Notebook, Speed Draft, Air Marshal, Counter Doctrine, Break Doctrine, Form Commitment, Full Compass, and Flicksmith.
