# Relic Pool

## Pool Shape

The first relic pool is roughly seventy percent route relics, twenty percent stacking relics, and ten percent strange relics.

Route relics keep the system stable. Stacking relics create growth. Strange relics create stories.

## 上行刀路首版遗物和契约

Phase 1 的遗物先服务三条路线：追击、闪反、爆发。每条路线两个遗物，目标是让三局手感能在一分钟内明显不同。遗物负责变味，契约负责高风险高收益。

| Name | Type | Route | Effect | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Redline Scabbard | Relic | Chase | Right flicks make the next tap auto-chase if it is played before the replacement cooldown finishes. | Repeated chase adds overheat. |
| Comet Sheath | Relic | Chase | Every fourth played card becomes a chase variant if the card supports it. | Chase variants recover slower when they miss. |
| Borrowed Edge | Relic | Counter | Perfect left-flick evade repeats the next Quick Slash once. | Late evade drops extra stance. |
| Mirror Scar | Relic | Counter | Left flicks leave a delayed afterimage that repeats the card's basic action. | Feints can bait the afterimage into whiffing. |
| Drop Hammer | Relic | Burst | Down flicks gain a shockwave if the boss is staggered or airborne. | Missed down flick locks one card briefly. |
| Execution Drum | Relic | Burst | Execute down flick hits harder during break windows and extends hit-stop. | Early Execute weakens the next break attempt. |
| Redline Pursuit | Contract | Chase | Right-flick replacement becomes faster for the next fight. | Boss gains backstep slash after repeated pursuit. |
| Mirror Oath | Contract | Counter | Perfect left-flick windows give stronger counter payoff. | Normal Guard absorbs less pressure. |
| Black Drum | Contract | Burst | Execute can trigger earlier during break pressure. | Missing a down flick locks one hand slot until the next replacement. |

契约不能被做成隐藏扣数值。玩家拿契约前必须能看到它强化什么动作，也能看到 Boss 或手牌会用什么方式惩罚失败。
